GOOD TO KNOW

FAQs – EnglandMeds

Is ordering online really safe?

Yes—your order is processed through a secure system, and every prescription is reviewed by a qualified UK pharmacist before anything is dispatched.

Do I need to visit a pharmacy in person?

No. You can upload your NHS or private prescription online and complete everything from home.

Will anyone know what’s inside my parcel?

No. Your order arrives in plain, unmarked packaging. Only you know what’s inside.

Can I trust the quality of the medicines?

Absolutely. All medicines come from authorized UK wholesalers and meet MHRA safety standards.

What if I need help or have questions?

Our support team and UK pharmacists are available to guide you. Just contact us by phone or email — we’re here to help.
