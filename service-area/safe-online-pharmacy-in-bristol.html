Bristol: A city overview

Bristol, a vibrant and historic city in the Southwest of England, is a hub of innovation, culture, and community spirit. With a population estimated at 494,400 in mid-2024 and projected to exceed half a million by mid-2025, Bristol is one of the fastest-growing core cities in England and Wales, experiencing an 11.4% growth over the last decade. This dynamic growth brings with it diverse health needs and challenges, which EnglandmedsUK is committed to addressing through accessible and reliable online pharmacy services. Key health data indicates several areas of focus for residents:

Population Health: The city has a relatively young demographic, with a median age of 33.4 years, younger than the national average. This youthful population contributes to higher rates of certain health conditions, such as sexually transmitted infections (STIs), with 3,640 new diagnoses in 2024

Lifestyle-Related Conditions: While adult obesity rates in Bristol (58.1%) are slightly lower than the England average (64.0%), they remain a significant public health concern. Smoking prevalence stands at 12.7%, similar to the national rate. Alcohol-related harm also presents a challenge, with 2,816 hospital admissions in 2023/24, a rate higher than the national average

Mental Health and Well-being: Mental health support is a growing priority, evidenced by a 17.7% increase in new depression diagnoses in 2023/24, affecting 9,479 patients. Access to timely and effective mental health services is crucial for the city's residents.

Preventative Care: Initiatives like the “Think Pharmacy First” campaign highlight the importance of community pharmacies in managing common health conditions such as sinusitis, sore throat, and uncomplicated UTIs, allowing residents to receive treatment without a GP appointment.
