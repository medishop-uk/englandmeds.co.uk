FAQ about online dispensary

Is it safe to buy medicine from an online dispensary?

Yes, if the dispensary is licensed and verified by health authorities. Always check reviews, certifications, and avoid sites that sell prescription drugs without a valid prescription.

Do I need a prescription to order medicines online?

For prescription-only medicines, a valid doctor’s prescription is required. Over-the-counter products can usually be purchased without any prescription.

How long does delivery take from an online dispensary?

Delivery time depends on your location and the service provider, but most offer same-day or 1–3 day delivery. Some platforms also provide express or emergency delivery options.

Are the prices at online dispensaries lower than those at local pharmacies?

Often yes, online dispensaries provide discounts, bundle deals, and lower operational costs. However, prices can vary, so it’s smart to compare before ordering.

Can I use my existing NHS prescription with MidlandsRxUK?

Yes, we accept both NHS and private prescriptions. If you already have a valid NHS prescription from your GP, you can submit it to us for dispensing.[c][d]
