How to check counterfeit drugs

Fake medicines can be risky because they may contain the wrong ingredients, incorrect strength, or poor-quality substances. Before using any sleeping tablet, customers should always check the packaging and product details carefully.

  • Check that the box or strip is properly sealed.
  • Look for the medicine name, strength, batch number, and expiry date.
  • Avoid tablets with broken, unclear, or unusual packaging.
  • Be careful of spelling mistakes on the label or box.
  • Do not buy from unknown sellers with no contact details.
  • Avoid prices that look unrealistically cheap.
  • Contact the pharmacy team if the product looks different from expected.

FAQ about sleeping pills

What are sleeping pills used for?

Sleeping pills are usually used to help people manage short-term sleep problems or insomnia. They may help a person fall asleep faster, stay asleep longer, or improve sleep quality when used correctly.

Can I buy sleeping pills online in the UK?

Yes, some sleeping pills can be ordered online through a trusted pharmacy service. Customers should always choose a reliable pharmacy, check product details, and use medicines responsibly.

Are sleeping pills safe to use every night?

Sleeping pills are not always suitable for long-term daily use because some may cause side effects, tolerance, or dependence. It is better to follow proper medical advice and avoid taking more than recommended.

How quickly do sleeping pills work?

The working time can vary depending on the type of medicine, strength, and the person using it. Some sleeping tablets may work within a short time, while others may take longer to show an effect.

What should I check before taking sleeping tablets?

Before taking sleeping tablets, check the medicine name, dosage, expiry date, packaging, and usage instructions. Avoid alcohol, do not mix with other sedative medicines unless advised, and contact a healthcare professional if you are unsure.
