Anxiety and panic disorder medicines

Anxiety and panic disorder medicines are used to reduce sudden fear, nervousness, restlessness, and panic symptoms. Common generic names include alprazolam, diazepam, clonazepam, sertraline, and escitalopram. 

Anxiety & seizure disorder medicines

These medicines may help manage anxiety symptoms as well as seizure-related conditions under proper medical guidance. Common generic names include carbamazepine, sodium valproate, lorazepam, and levetiracetam. 

Nerve pain & neuropathy medicines

Nerve pain and neuropathy medicines are used for burning, tingling, shooting pain, and nerve-related discomfort. Common generic names include pregabalin, gabapentin, amitriptyline, duloxetine, and carbamazepine.

How to detect counterfeit medications

Fake medicines can be unsafe, ineffective, or harmful, so checking authenticity is very important before using any product. A few simple checks can help you avoid poor-quality or suspicious medicines.

  • Check if the packaging is sealed and not damaged.
  • Look for a clear product name, strength, batch number, and expiry date.
  • Avoid medicines with spelling mistakes or unclear printing.
  • Do not buy from unknown sellers with no contact details.
  • Compare the product appearance with trusted pharmacy information.
  • Be careful of prices that look unusually cheap.
  • Contact the pharmacy team if the tablet, strip, or box looks different.
