Side effects of modafinil

Most people tolerate Modafinil well when it is used exactly as prescribed. However, like all medicines, it can cause side effects that range from mild discomfort to more serious reactions in some individuals. The likelihood and severity of side effects can vary depending on the dose, age, medical history, and other medicines being taken at the same time.

  • Headache: One of the most commonly reported side effects after taking Modafinil.
  • Difficulty sleeping: Taking it too late in the day may make it harder to fall asleep at night.
  • Nausea: Some people may experience an upset stomach or mild nausea.
  • Dizziness: A feeling of light-headedness can occur, especially when starting treatment.
  • Anxiety or nervousness: Modafinil may increase restlessness or feelings of nervousness in certain individuals.
