Side effect of alprazolam

Alprazolam can cause side effects, especially when the dose is high, taken with alcohol, combined with other sedatives, or used without proper medical advice.
If side effects feel severe, unusual, or dangerous, patients should stop and seek urgent medical help or speak with a doctor or pharmacist.

  • Drowsiness and tiredness: Alprazolam may make you sleepy, slow, or less alert.
  • Dizziness and poor coordination: It may affect balance, movement, and reaction time.
  • Confusion or memory issues: Some people may feel forgetful, unfocused, or mentally unclear.
  • Mood or behaviour changes: Rarely, benzodiazepines may cause agitation, low mood, or unusual behaviour.
  • Dependence and withdrawal: Regular or incorrect use may lead to dependence, and stopping suddenly may cause withdrawal symptoms.
