Side effects of zopiclone

Zopiclone is commonly prescribed for short-term treatment of insomnia, but it can cause side effects in some people. Most reactions are mild and often improve as the body adjusts to the medication.

  • Bitter or metallic taste: A very common effect that may remain for several hours after taking the tablet.
  • Dry mouth: Some users experience reduced saliva production and mouth discomfort.
  • Daytime drowsiness: Sleepiness or reduced alertness may continue into the next day.
  • Dizziness: You may feel light-headed, especially when getting up quickly.
  • Headache: Mild to moderate headaches can occur during treatment.
