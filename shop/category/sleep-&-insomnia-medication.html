FAQ about sleep & insomnia medicines

What medicines are commonly used for insomnia?

Doctors may prescribe short-term sleeping tablets, sedative medicines, or other treatments depending on the cause of insomnia and the patient’s health condition.

Can I buy sleep medicines online in the UK?

Yes, but prescription sleep medicines should only be purchased from a trusted pharmacy after proper medical approval.

Are insomnia medicines safe for daily use?

Some sleep medicines are meant for short-term use only, as regular use may cause dependence, tolerance, or unwanted side effects.

How long do sleep medicines take to work?

Many sleep medicines may start working within a short time, but the exact effect depends on the medicine, dosage, and individual response.

Can I stop taking insomnia medicine suddenly?

You should not stop certain sleep medicines suddenly without medical advice, as this may cause withdrawal symptoms or rebound insomnia.
