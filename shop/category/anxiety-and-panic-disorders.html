FAQ about anxiety and panic disorder medicines

What medicines are commonly used for anxiety and panic disorder?

Doctors may prescribe SSRIs, SNRIs, beta blockers, or short-term calming medicines depending on the patient’s symptoms and medical history.

How long do anxiety medicines take to work?

Some anxiety medicines may take a few weeks to show full results, while fast-acting medicines may work sooner when prescribed for short-term use.

Can panic disorder medicines stop panic attacks?

Certain prescribed medicines can help reduce the frequency and intensity of panic attacks, but treatment works best with proper medical guidance.

Are anxiety medicines safe to take every day?

Some anxiety medicines are designed for daily use, while others should only be used short term because they may cause dependence or side effects.

Can I stop taking anxiety medicine suddenly?

No, many anxiety and panic medicines should not be stopped suddenly, as this may cause withdrawal symptoms or make anxiety worse.
