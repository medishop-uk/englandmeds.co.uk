FAQ about anxiety & seizure disorder medicines

What medicines are used for anxiety and seizure disorders?

Doctors may prescribe antidepressants, beta blockers, benzodiazepines, or anti-seizure medicines depending on the condition and patient history.

Can one medicine treat both anxiety and seizures?

Some medicines may help with both anxiety symptoms and seizure control, but they should only be used under medical supervision.

Are anxiety and seizure medicines safe to take daily?

Some are suitable for daily use, while others may cause dependence or side effects if used long term without proper guidance.

Can I buy anxiety and seizure medicines online in the UK?

Yes, but prescription-only medicines should be purchased only through a trusted pharmacy with proper medical approval.

Can I stop anxiety or seizure medicine suddenly?

No, stopping suddenly may cause withdrawal symptoms, seizure risk, or worsening anxiety, so always follow medical advice.
